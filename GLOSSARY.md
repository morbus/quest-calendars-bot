# GLOSSARY.md

A Discord game in which **Players** advance through solo, dice-driven role-playing **Quests** one **Page** per day.

## Language

**Player**:
A Discord user who plays one or more **Quests**.
_Avoid_: User.

**Quest**:
A named, authored adventure made of many sequential **Pages**, which a **Player** plays as one **Character**.
It may be published a few **Pages** at a time and is finished only when its author marks it complete.
_Avoid_: Adventure, calendar, campaign, story.

**Page**:
One day's entry in a **Quest**: the scene, the **Actions** the **Player** takes, and their **Outcomes**.
_Avoid_: Calendar page, day, entry.

**Character**:
A playable persona defined by a **Quest**, with starting **Stats**, **Traits**, and **Abilities**.
_Avoid_: Avatar, hero.

**Stat**:
One of the values a **Character** uses mainly in combat: Health and Max Health,
a Defense modifier, an Attack modifier, and a Damage die to roll after a successful attack.
_Avoid_: Attribute, trait.

**Trait**:
One of a **Character**'s bonus modifiers added to rolls, such as
Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intellect, Wisdom, or Charisma.
_Avoid_: Ability score, attribute, stat.

**Ability**:
A named rule of a **Character** that modifies rolls or **Outcomes**, either always on or limited
per **Page** or per **Rest**; limited **Abilities** are used after seeing the roll.
_Avoid_: Power, skill, special ability.

**Rest**:
A break in which the **Character** recovers, either because a **Page** grants one or because the **Player**
uses a limited item to take one at the start or end of a **Page**. It refills Health, Spell Points, and per-Rest
**Abilities** on the **Sheet**, clears conditions such as Poisoned, and allows changing equipment and crafting.
_Avoid_: Camp, long rest, recovery.

**Sheet**:
The current state of one **Player**'s **Character** in one **Quest**:
**Stats**, **Traits**, **Abilities**, currency, equipment, items, and conditions.
_Avoid_: Character sheet, profile.

**Action**:
One thing the **Player** does on a **Page**, usually a die roll plus
a **Stat** or **Trait**, whose result determines the **Outcome**.
Numbered **Actions** are all done in sequential order.
_Avoid_: Encounter, option, task.

**Outcome**:
The result an **Action** reaches, chosen by which band ("8 to 10", "15 or more") the final
roll falls in: prose plus **Effects**, and sometimes further **Actions** or **Choices**.
_Avoid_: Resolution, result.

**Effect**:
One change an **Outcome** makes to the **Sheet** or to later rolls, such as losing Health, gaining currency
or an item, a bonus on the next **Action** or the next **Page**, or a **Note**. "Tomorrow" in **Page** text
means the **Player**'s next **Page** in that **Quest**, not the next **Game Day**.
_Avoid_: Consequence, modifier.

**Note**:
A named marker an **Outcome** writes on the **Sheet**
(e.g. "Chunk of Amber") so that later **Pages** can check for it.
_Avoid_: Flag, "written down".

**Choice**:
A set of lettered **Actions** on a **Page** from which the **Player** picks exactly one.

**Game Day**:
The calendar day, rolling over at midnight in the game's single timezone,
on which a **Player** earns one more **Page** in each **Quest** they are playing.
_Avoid_: Quest Day.

**Backlog**:
The **Pages** a **Player** has earned in a **Quest** but not yet played;
it holds at most 14, and days beyond that do not add to it.
_Avoid_: Catch-up, missed days.

**Announcement**:
A public, spoiler-free note in a Discord channel that a **Player** has finished
a **Page**: the **Page**'s **Teaser** plus a tally of the **Effects** they received.

**Teaser**:
A one-line, spoiler-free summary of a **Page**, written by its author for **Announcements**.

**Rule Module**:
An optional set of game mechanics, such as the Sunday Lottery, Minions, or
Crafting, that a **Quest** turns on; the core rules apply to every **Quest**.

## Relationships

- A **Player** may play multiple **Quests** at once, each from its own first **Page**, and their progress is the same on every Discord server.
- A **Player** picks one of a **Quest**'s **Characters** when starting it, and has one **Sheet** per **Quest**.
- A **Page** contains one or more **Actions**, some grouped into **Choices**.
- A **Quest** contains many **Pages** in a fixed order.
- Each **Game Day** adds one **Page** to the **Player**'s **Backlog** for each **Quest** they are playing, up to 14.
- Finishing a **Page** produces one **Announcement**; the **Page**'s content is visible only to that **Player**.
