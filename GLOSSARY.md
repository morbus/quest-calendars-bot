# The Daily Quest

A Discord game in which **Players** advance through solo, dice-driven role-playing **Quests** one **Page** per day.

## Language

**Ability**:
A **Character**'s skill or power that modifies rolls or **Outcomes**, either always on or limited per **Page** or per Rest; limited **Abilities** are used after seeing the roll.
_Avoid_: Skill, power, special ability

**Action**:
One thing the **Player** does on a **Page**, usually a roll of a die plus a trait or stat, whose result selects an **Outcome**.
_Avoid_: Task, encounter, option

**Announcement**:
A public, spoiler-free note in a channel that a **Player** has finished a **Page**: the **Page**'s **Teaser** plus a tally of the **Effects** they received.

**Backlog**:
The **Pages** a **Player** has earned in a **Quest** but not yet played; it holds at most 14, and days beyond that do not add to it.
_Avoid_: Catch-up, missed days

**Character**:
A playable persona defined by a **Quest**, with starting traits, stats, and **Abilities**, such as Ivy Bushtail or SD-247.
_Avoid_: Hero, villain (as a general term), avatar

**Choice**:
A set of lettered **Actions** on a **Page** from which the **Player** picks exactly one; numbered **Actions** are all done in order.

**Effect**:
One change an **Outcome** makes to the **Sheet** or to later rolls, such as losing Health, gaining currency or an item, a bonus on the next **Action** or the next **Page**, or a **Note**. "Tomorrow" in **Page** text means the **Player**'s next **Page** in that **Quest**, not the next calendar day.
_Avoid_: Consequence, modifier

**Game Day**:
The calendar day, rolling over at midnight in the game's single timezone, on which a **Player** earns one more **Page** in each **Quest** they are playing.
_Avoid_: Quest Day

**Note**:
A named marker an **Outcome** writes on the **Sheet** (e.g. "Chunk of Amber", "Grateful Companion") so that later **Pages** can check for it.
_Avoid_: Flag, "written down"

**Outcome**:
The result an **Action** reaches, chosen by which band ("8 to 10", "15 or more") the final roll falls in: prose plus **Effects**, and sometimes further **Actions** or **Choices**.
_Avoid_: Result, resolution, back page

**Page**:
One day's entry in a **Quest**: the scene, the **Actions** the **Player** takes, and their **Outcomes**.
_Avoid_: Day, entry, calendar page

**Player**:
A Discord user who plays one or more **Quests**.
_Avoid_: User, hero

**Quest**:
A named, authored adventure made of many sequential **Pages**, which a **Player** plays as one **Character**. It may be published a few **Pages** at a time and is finished only when its author marks it complete.
_Avoid_: Story, campaign, adventure, calendar

**Rule Module**:
An optional set of game mechanics, such as Minions, Crafting, or the Sunday Lottery, that a **Quest** turns on; the core rules apply to every **Quest**.

**Sheet**:
The current state of one **Player**'s **Character** in one **Quest**: traits, stats, currency, items, equipment, and conditions.
_Avoid_: Character sheet, villain sheet, profile

**Teaser**:
A one-line, spoiler-free summary of a **Page**, written by its author for **Announcements**.

## Relationships

- A **Player** may be playing many **Quests** at once, each from its own Day 1, and their progress is the same on every Discord server
- A **Player** picks one of a **Quest**'s **Characters** when starting it, and has one **Sheet** per **Quest**
- A **Page** contains one or more **Actions**, some grouped into **Choices**
- A **Quest** contains many **Pages** in a fixed order
- Each **Game Day** adds one **Page** to the **Player**'s **Backlog** for each **Quest** they are playing, up to 14
- Finishing a **Page** produces one **Announcement**; the **Page**'s content is visible only to that **Player**
