# The Daily Quest

A Discord game in which **Players** advance through solo, dice-driven role-playing **Stories** one **Quest** per day.

## Language

**Ability**:
A **Character**'s skill or power that modifies rolls or **Outcomes**, either always on or limited per **Quest** or per Rest; limited **Abilities** are used after seeing the roll.
_Avoid_: Skill, power, special ability

**Action**:
One thing the **Player** does in a **Quest**, usually a roll of a die plus a trait or stat, whose result selects an **Outcome**.
_Avoid_: Task, encounter, option

**Announcement**:
A public, spoiler-free note in a channel that a **Player** has finished a **Quest**: the **Quest**'s **Teaser** plus a tally of the **Effects** they received.

**Backlog**:
The **Quests** a **Player** has earned in a **Story** but not yet played; it holds at most 14, and days beyond that do not add to it.
_Avoid_: Catch-up, missed days

**Character**:
A playable persona defined by a **Story**, with starting traits, stats and **Abilities**, such as Ivy Bushtail or SD-247.
_Avoid_: Hero, villain (as a general term), avatar

**Choice**:
A set of lettered **Actions** in a **Quest** from which the **Player** picks exactly one; numbered **Actions** are all done in order.

**Effect**:
One change an **Outcome** makes to the **Sheet** or to later rolls, such as losing Health, gaining currency or an item, a bonus on the next **Action** or the next **Quest**, or a **Note**. "Tomorrow" in **Quest** text means the **Player**'s next **Quest** in that **Story**, not the next calendar day.
_Avoid_: Consequence, modifier

**Note**:
A named marker an **Outcome** writes on the **Sheet** (e.g. "Chunk of Amber", "Grateful Companion") so that later **Quests** can check for it.
_Avoid_: Flag, "written down"

**Outcome**:
The result an **Action** reaches, chosen by which band ("8 to 10", "15 or more") the final roll falls in: prose plus **Effects**, and sometimes further **Actions** or **Choices**.
_Avoid_: Result, resolution, back page

**Player**:
A Discord user who plays one or more **Stories**.
_Avoid_: User, hero

**Quest**:
One day's entry in a **Story**: the scene, the **Actions** the **Player** takes, and their **Outcomes**. The printed rules' "page" (as in "Once per Page") means a **Quest**.
_Avoid_: Page, day, entry

**Quest Day**:
The calendar day, rolling over at midnight in the game's single timezone, on which a **Player** earns one more **Quest** in each **Story** they are playing.

**Rule Module**:
An optional set of game mechanics, such as Minions, Crafting or the Sunday Lottery, that a **Story** turns on; the core rules apply to every **Story**.

**Sheet**:
The current state of one **Player**'s **Character** in one **Story**: traits, stats, currency, items, equipment and conditions.
_Avoid_: Character sheet, villain sheet, profile

**Story**:
A named, authored adventure made of many sequential **Quests**, which a **Player** plays as one **Character**. It may be published a few **Quests** at a time and is finished only when its author marks it complete.
_Avoid_: Campaign, adventure, calendar, many-day quest

**Teaser**:
A one-line, spoiler-free summary of a **Quest**, written by its author for **Announcements**.

## Relationships

- A **Player** may be playing many **Stories** at once, each from its own Day 1, and their progress is the same on every Discord server
- A **Player** picks one of a **Story**'s **Characters** when starting it, and has one **Sheet** per **Story**
- A **Quest** contains one or more **Actions**, some grouped into **Choices**
- A **Story** contains many **Quests** in a fixed order
- Each **Quest Day** adds one **Quest** to the **Player**'s **Backlog** for each **Story** they are playing, up to 14
- Finishing a **Quest** produces one **Announcement**; the **Quest**'s content is visible only to that **Player**
